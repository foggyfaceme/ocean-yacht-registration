Frequently Asked Questions

How long does yacht registration take?

Processing times vary by registry. Poland can take as little as 2-5 days, while UK Part 1 or Cayman Islands might take 2-4 weeks depending on documentation.

Do I need a survey for registration?

It depends on the flag state and vessel size/age. Poland generally does not require a survey for private yachts under 24m. We will guide you based on your specific case.

Can I register my yacht if I am not an EU citizen?

Yes. We can set up a dormant company (e.g., UK LTD or Delaware LLC) to hold the yacht, allowing non-EU citizens to register under EU flags.
